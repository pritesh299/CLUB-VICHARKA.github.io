VICHARAKA

THE ROBOTICS CLUB

ABOUT US

Vicharak is a robotics club at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) created to facilitate the interest and projects of undergraduate students passionate about robotics. Established in 2024 by a group of first-year and second-year students, the club started as a team participating in a rover-building competition and later developed into a club with a dedicated lab for robotics-related activities.

Vicharak is the only club at IISc with such a lab, providing a space where students can work on projects that align with their interests. The club is funded by IISc, Robot Bosch, and ART PARK, offering members access to resources and equipment to help bring their ideas to life.

Vicharak creates an environment that encourages learning, collaboration, and hands-on experience in robotics, empowering students to pursue their personal projects and explore their passion for the field.
